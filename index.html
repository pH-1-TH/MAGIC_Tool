EIT Engineering Note

According to Metropolitan Electricity Authority (MEA) and Provincial Electricity Authority (PEA) regulations, the main circuit breaker on the secondary side should be sized to protect the transformer from overload while accounting for inrush currents. Standard impedance for distribution transformers (500 kVA - 2500 kVA) is typically 6.0%. Always verify short-circuit ratings (Icu) of breakers against the calculated Isc.

Professional Guidance

This tool uses the Point-to-Point method which provides a conservative estimate of short-circuit current. It assumes a symmetrical fault and ignores motor contribution and source impedance beyond the available fault current input. For mission-critical coordination studies, use software that supports full IEC 60909 or ANSI/IEEE C37 complex impedance matrix analysis.
